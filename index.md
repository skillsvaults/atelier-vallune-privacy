# Privacy Policy — Atelier Vallune

**Last updated: October 2, 2026**

Atelier Vallune uses eBay APIs to operate its seller account and manage listings, inventory, orders, and related seller functions when authorized by the account holder.

## Information accessed

Depending on the permissions granted, the application may access eBay account, listing, inventory, order, fulfillment, messaging, financial, dispute, and related information made available through eBay APIs.

## Use and sharing

This information is used only to operate and support the Atelier Vallune eBay seller account. It is not sold or shared with unrelated third parties. eBay processes information under its own privacy policy and terms.

## Storage and security

Access is limited to the authorized operator and systems required to provide the seller-account functions. Credentials are stored using access-controlled secret storage. Reasonable safeguards are used to prevent unauthorized access.

## Retention and deletion

Information is retained only as needed to operate the seller account and meet applicable legal obligations. The account holder may revoke the application's access through eBay. Requests about data handled by this application can be sent through the eBay account used to authorize it.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact Atelier Vallune through its eBay seller account.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the application's data practices change. The current version is published at this URL.
